The Problem

Healthcare Monitoring Is Broken

Emergency triage and continuous monitoring rely on contact sensors, cloud dependency, and manual intervention — failing exactly when they're needed most.

🩺

Contact-Based & Slow

Vital monitoring requires multiple physical sensors applied to the patient. In mass-casualty and emergency scenarios, this becomes a critical bottleneck.

⚡

Labour-Intensive Monitoring

Continuous patient monitoring is uncomfortable for patients and burdens clinical staff, limiting scalability in high-volume care environments.

☁️

Cloud-Dependent Systems

Camera-based alternatives depend on stable cloud infrastructure and optimal lighting — unreliable in field emergencies, pandemics, or remote deployments.

🚨

No Rapid Triage Tool

Emergency responders lack fast, non-contact tools to visualize inflammation and injury severity before physical intervention. Every second matters.

Our Solution

AyurDrishti Changes
Everything

A thermal AI platform that monitors vital signs and detects inflammation — without contact, cloud dependency, or privacy compromise.

🌡️

5 Vitals, Non-Contact

Heart rate, temperature, SpO₂, respiratory rate, and blood pressure estimated using thermal video alone — no physical sensors required.

🔥

Inflammation-Based Triage

Thermal imaging reveals regions, spread, centre, and relative severity of inflammation — providing physiological insight before physical examination begins.

⚡

Edge AI Processing

All AI inference runs directly on embedded hardware. No cloud required. Fully functional in disaster zones, ambulances, and remote facilities.

🔒

Privacy-Preserving

Thermal imaging captures physiological data without facial recognition or identifiable imagery — compliant with healthcare privacy requirements by design.

🏥

Built for Every Setting

Designed for hospitals, ambulances, emergency response, care homes, sports injury, and remote healthcare — one platform, multiple deployment contexts.

Workflow

From Capture to Clinical Insight in Seconds

Six automated stages, running entirely on the edge device, deliver real-time vitals and triage indicators without a clinician touching the patient.

Current Technology Stack

Thermal Cameras Computer Vision Signal Processing Edge AI Embedded Hardware Cloud Dashboard
01

Capture Thermal Video

A thermal camera streams high-resolution infrared video of the patient. Works in any lighting condition — bright rooms, dark ambulances, outdoor emergencies.

02

Detect Region of Interest

Computer vision algorithms automatically identify and isolate anatomically relevant zones — face, chest, wrist — for targeted physiological signal extraction.

03

Extract Thermal Signals

Subtle temperature variations — driven by blood flow, respiration, and cardiovascular activity — are extracted as time-series physiological signals per ROI.

04

Apply Signal Processing & AI Models

Optimised DSP filters and trained neural network models run on the edge device, cleaning noise and mapping thermal signals to physiological biomarkers.

05

Compute Vitals & Inflammation Patterns

Five vital signs are estimated. Simultaneously, thermal asymmetry analysis maps inflammation zones, severity gradients, and impact centres for triage decisions.

06

Display in Real Time

Results stream to an on-device display and optional cloud dashboard — annotated thermal view, vital readouts, triage indicators, and trend graphs, all live.

Competitive Advantage

Why Thermal Imaging Wins

Existing camera-based systems and sensor rigs both fail in the field. AyurDrishti is built for where healthcare actually happens.

❌ Existing Systems Struggle With

⚠️

Lighting Variability — RGB cameras fail in low light, backlit, or night-time emergency scenarios where decisions are most critical.

⚠️

Cloud Dependence — Most AI health monitors require constant internet. Disaster zones and rural settings have neither bandwidth nor reliability.

⚠️

Privacy Violations — Facial recognition systems capture and process identifiable patient imagery, creating regulatory and ethical exposure.

⚠️

No Triage Capability — Contact sensors and RGB cameras provide vitals only — they cannot reveal inflammation, injury distribution, or severity patterns.

✓ AyurDrishti Advantages

✓

All-Condition Operation — Thermal imaging is inherently ambient-light independent. Works identically in darkness, bright sunlight, or smoke-filled environments.

✓

True Edge AI — Full inference runs on the embedded device. No cloud, no latency, no connectivity requirement. Operational anywhere on Earth.

✓

No Facial Recognition — Thermal physiology captures body heat patterns, not faces. Privacy-preserving by architectural design, not policy.

✓

Thermal Triage Intelligence — Unique inflammation mapping provides actionable severity insight before any physical examination — a world first in deployable edge form.
